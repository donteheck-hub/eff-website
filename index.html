LEAGUE OPERATIONS

EFF Rulebook

Everything you need to run and play an official EFF game, from start-of-game procedure through penalties, special teams, fumbles, overtime, and league operations.

Start of Game

Force start is 15 minutes after the scheduled game time.

  • If neither team has 6 players, the game results in a double FFL.
  • If one team has 6 players and the other has fewer than 6, a 5 minute extension may be granted — but only if the team with 6 players agrees to it.
  • The maximum number of players allowed in game is 8 players.

Penalties

Both head coaches are responsible for obeying the rulebook and going by the referees call.

Illegal Formation (IF)

5 Yds
  • In either formation, no receiver may line up in or on the hashes.
  • There are no running backs — a handoff to a wide receiver will result in an illegal formation.

Formation-specific requirements are detailed in the section below.

Offensive Formation · 1C 2TE

Season 4

The approved offensive formation uses 1 Center and 2 Tight Ends.

  • Team of 6 players - 1 Center and if you want to run, 1/2 Tight Ends or none
  • Team of 7 players - 1 Center and 1/2 Tight Ends
  • Team of 8 players - 1 Center and 2 Tight Ends

Force Eligibility: If you are forcing a team and decide to remove a player, you must announce the change using the :H command so the opposing team knows you are now eligible to run the ball.

Improper Block (IB)

5 Yds

Called when a tight end, in either formation, blocks more than 5 yards behind the line of scrimmage.

Lag Sack (LS)

+5 Yds & 1st down

Called when a defensive player lags and sacks the quarterback.

  • Determined by how far the defensive end is from the quarterback — for example, if the DE falls well before or after the QB, or if no DE falls but the QB is still sacked.
  • The offending player must also be given a lag warning.

Too Many Blockers (TMB)

5 Yds & loss of down

Called when a wide receiver intentionally blocks a defender:

  • A wr intentionally moves toward the DE to block them (forward or backward).
  • A line-of-scrimmage wr lines up in front of the DE and blocks forward or backward (does not apply to DBs).
  • A wr stays on the los blocking backwards — this is not considered an off-jam and will be TMB (if the wr is simply held, it does not count).
  • A wr blocks forward against the defender first. If they block each other at the same time, it is not considered TMB.

It is not TMB if the wr blocks backwards. TMB can only be called if the quarterback throws the ball after the block — the flag will not count if the QB runs the ball.

Grounding (GR)

15 Yds & loss of down

This flag is mostly a judgment call — if a receiver isn't within about 15 yards of the ball, it will be considered grounding, unless the wr and QB clearly had a miscommunication or the wr cut away.

  • If the line of scrimmage is within 20 yards of the endzone, the QB may throw it out of bounds at the back endzone. The QB must be within the hashes — once they step out, the flag is valid.
  • If unsure, the referee may pause for a challenge review and neither team will be penalized due to the review.

Center Rollout (CR)

5 Yds & loss of down

Called when a center rolls out past the line of scrimmage, regardless of formation, before the ball is thrown.

  • Passes to the center will be considered grounding.
  • Applies if the center passes the los in an attempt to block (does not count on runs).

Too Many Men (TMM)

±5 Yds

Called when either team has 9 players on the field after the hike.

  • Offense has 9 → -5 yards
  • Defense has 9 → +5 yards

Ineligible Receiver (Inel)

Incomplete

Called when a wr/te steps out of bounds or on the white border before the ball is thrown.

  • The play is ruled incomplete — line it back to the previous play at the same first-down line. Do not reset the down to the play before; they should have lost a down.

Special Teams

No fake field goals or punts are allowed.

Onsides

  • Must be under 3 minutes in the 4th quarter.
  • Teams cannot go for an onside kick in a tie game.
  • Teams are allowed to go ineligible.
  • Teams are allowed to block anyone.
  • Admins may not teleport a player to gain an advantage — this results in an FFL and a suspension if it causes a win.

Auto Punts

You must punt if the LOS is within your own endzone to the 50 and it is 4th & 8 or longer. Not applicable under 2 minutes in the 2nd or 4th quarter.

  • Example: you're at your own 20, 4th & 15 in the 1st quarter — this is a force. If it were 1:40 left in the 2nd quarter, it would not be forced.
  • At the opposing 45 with auto FG/punt rules in effect, you may choose between a 62-yard field goal or a punt.

Fumbles

Only specific players are able to fumble:

  • The quarterback can fumble at all times.
  • Punt / kick returners can fumble at all times.
  • Onside returners can fumble at all times.
  • A wide receiver can fumble if they catch the ball behind the line of scrimmage.
  • A defensive back can fumble once they pass the line of scrimmage (the offense's side).

Milking

  • Quarterbacks must hike before the play clock hits 10 seconds, unless there is 1 minute left in the 2nd or 4th quarter.
  • Under 1 minute in the 2nd or 4th quarter, if the defense is trailing: if the QB steps back 30+ yards, the referee must stop the clock.
  • Under 1 minute in the 2nd or 4th quarter, if the defense is trailing: if the offense is tackled or sacked behind the los, the clock will stop.

QBH Glitch

If the game shows a fumble on-screen after the quarterback is hit by a defensive end, but something else actually occurs (such as a sack or a throw), the play stands as what the game ruled it — completion, interception, or sack.

Overtime

Format

  • A coin toss at the beginning of the overtime period determines who starts each period.
  • If a team kicks off, they get the ball second.
  • If a team receives, they get the ball first.
  • Each possession starts at the opposing team's 35-yard line.
  • Each possession goes until one team ends up with a lower score than the other.

Scoring

2-point conversions and PATs are all on the table.

  • If Team A scores 7 and Team B scores 8, the game is over.
  • If Team A scores 7 and Team B scores 6, the game is over.
  • If Team A scores 7 and Team B scores 7, the game goes to OT2.
  • Once the teams reach OT3, they must go for 2 after every touchdown.

Mercy Rule

Once a team is winning by 40 or more points, mercy applies and the referee must end the game instantly.

Operations

Referees

  • Must stay focused on the game the entire time.
  • Must have the correct settings on.
  • Must accurately call flags (may look back at the rulebook).
  • Must comply with league operations staff and challenges.

Streamers

  • Be present — change camera angles and commentate (commentary is optional).
  • Have VODs turned on.
  • Make sure the link to the stream is posted for league operations to review.

Head Coaches / in-game admins

  • You may not newplay at any time throughout the game.
  • You must listen to the referee and make things clear to them, such as challenges, no-reps, and substitutions.
  • If you question the referee's call, you may use an Ultimate Challenge with the correct format — otherwise it will not be considered.
  • Any admin abuse (this also applies to refs) may result in penalties (-5 Yards / loss of down) or suspension.
